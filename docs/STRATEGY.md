# Strategy: Aspenridge Heating & Air (Phase 1, 2026-09-28)

1. **Brand:** Aspenridge Heating & Air. It names the aspen-covered ridges west of Denver and doesn't imitate a national brand. `aspenridgeair.com` returned RDAP 404. Run a USPTO Class 37 search and a Colorado entity search.
2. **Market:** Denver metro, Colorado. It was chosen from LeadSmart HVAC Call payouts: Colorado averages $69.87 across 380 ZIPs, and Denver-metro ZIPs pay $84–97.65. The climate supports year-round demand: hot, dry summers; cold snaps; hail; altitude; and wildfire smoke.
3. **Voice and design:** plain and safety-first. Deep slate, evergreen, and copper CTAs, with a mountain-ridge and aspen-leaf mark.
4. **SERP (not a live scrape):** local packs dominated by large Denver HVAC brands. The organic gap is city-level housing content and Denver-specific topics (swamp coolers, altitude, hail, heat pump rebates).
5. **Keyword → page:** "hvac/ac repair denver" → home and service pages; "{service} {city}" → city or city+service pages; "swamp cooler repair denver" → `/hvac-services/evaporative-coolers/`; problem queries → guides.
6–8. **IA and locations:** state → 12 cities → gated city+service pages (`lib/quality.ts`: at least 180 and 90 local words).
9. **Homepage:** hero, urgent banner, categories, symptom finder, local knowledge (dry heat at altitude, 40-degree swings, heat pump incentives), service area with ZIP checker, guides, FAQ.
10. **PPC:** `/lp/ac-repair` (May–Sep), `/lp/furnace-repair` (Oct–Mar), `/lp/ac-replacement`. Bid first on the $97 ZIPs (Aurora 80010/11/14/16, Littleton 80122/25/28, Denver 80210/14/15/20/21/33/37).
11–14. **Linking, tech, SEO and leads:** as in the playbook (B9, B7).
